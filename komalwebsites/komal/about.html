With **over two decades of professional experience** at the absolute forefront of corporate development, Komal Khandge has engineered an unconventional, highly impactful career blueprint. Her multi-dimensional perspective is driven home through her work as a master **community builder** and creative **event curator**. Managing both premium commercial operations and deep social care networks through parent holding brands like *Sabka Global Corp*[cite: 1], her execution footprint connects high-level organizational structure seamlessly with profound grassroots realities.
